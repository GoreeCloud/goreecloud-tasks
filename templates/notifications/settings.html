Delivery boundary

Reminder dispatch re-checks task authorization immediately before publishing. If your access to a shared task has been revoked, the pending reminder is cancelled instead of sending task content after your access ends.

Only the task title, due time when present, and project name when present are sent. Descriptions, comments, labels, blockers, recovery notes, related records, and other detailed task content are intentionally excluded from the ntfy message.
